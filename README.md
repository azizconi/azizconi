## About

I am a Mobile Engineer with **5+ years of experience** building and maintaining production mobile products. I specialize in Android while expanding product delivery across platforms with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform. I care about clear architecture, responsive interfaces, predictable state, and software that remains easy to change after the first release.

- Building marketplace experiences at **Paydo Global**
- Previously worked on **MegaFon Life** at MegaFon Tajikistan
- Exploring shared product development with **Kotlin Multiplatform** and **Compose Multiplatform**
- Based in Dushanbe, Tajikistan · open to strong remote and hybrid teams
