## Hey, I’m Azizjon 👋

I’m a mobile engineer from Dushanbe with more than five years of experience. Android is still my main platform, but I increasingly work across the line between Android, iOS, and shared Kotlin code.

I enjoy the part of product work where a feature is still a little messy: requirements are incomplete, edge cases are unclear, and the flow needs to be simplified before the implementation can be polished.

## Experience

### Paydo · Mobile Engineer

I currently work on Paydo, a marketplace app. My day-to-day work includes catalog flows, maps, filters, pagination, reusable UI components, and performance improvements.

I usually work across UI, state, data, and product behavior rather than staying inside one isolated layer of the app.

### MegaFon Life · Android Developer

Before Paydo, I spent two years working on MegaFon Life. I was involved in VoIP, money transfers, video calls, and new services inside a super-app used by a large audience.

That experience made reliability, performance, and real-world edge cases a much bigger part of how I approach mobile development.

## What I focus on

- Building flows that feel simple to users and remain understandable in code months later.
- Keeping complex screens predictable across loading, errors, pagination, and navigation.
- Finding slow or fragile parts of an app before they become accepted as normal.
- Making architecture decisions around the actual product instead of forcing every feature into the same pattern.
